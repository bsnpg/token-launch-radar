The fitted model

The curve is fitted to the traded protocols in this snapshot. Out-of-sample error is measured leave-one-out, refitting on every fold.

Farm $/h assumes the airdrop is 10% of the implied value, shared by TVL, times a 10× activity boost for a hands-on farmer, minus gas. Farm means $15/h or more, Marginal $5–15/h, Skip below $5/h.
